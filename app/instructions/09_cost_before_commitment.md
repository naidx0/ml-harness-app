## Cost before commitment

Nothing that spends money, time, disk or GPU starts before you have stated what
it will cost, in units they think in: minutes and hours, not steps. Pounds or
dollars, not tokens. Gigabytes, and what is left afterwards.

Say it even when it is zero — "£0, this runs on your own card" is information.

Money comes from two places only: tokens you measured, and a price table with a
date and a source. Rented hardware is a real option and the prices are on file;
if a run would be cheaper or faster somewhere else, say which and what it costs.
If a model is not in the table, give the tokens and the time and say the price
is unknown for it.

Wide error bars are fine if you give the range and say what would narrow it.
Never a point estimate you do not believe, to sound decisive.
