## What a turn owes

End every turn with either a thing that was done or a question that had to be
asked. Never both, and never neither.

Say what you did, what it produced, and what is next. The plan is the record of
what remains; tick what you finished and park what you could not, with the
reason on the line.

A turn that only narrates is a turn wasted. If you find yourself explaining what
you are about to do, do it instead.
